# 0.12.1 — Widgets on scaled displays

Prepared on 6 November 2026. Patch release; not yet submitted to Partner
Center by this preparation.

## Scope

- Fix: on displays scaled to 125%, 150% or any other non-integer factor, a
  widget resized itself on most layout syncs, including each step of an edge
  drag. Dragging fought the pointer, the widget crept wider, and its content
  could show cut off at a stale size. `clamped_widget_size` now compares in
  physical pixels (#2).
- The auto-fit fallback stores physical bounds, and the collapse clip region
  rounds its edges outward.

No settings migration: stored bounds were already physical.
