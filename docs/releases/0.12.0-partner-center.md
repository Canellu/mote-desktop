### Description

```text
Mote Desktop is a Windows app for controlling compatible Philips Hue lights and accessories over your local network.

Core lighting controls and PC Sync are free, including one desktop widget for a room, zone, or light that folds into an animated moth, a named pill, or a round bubble. Mote Pro adds four more moth styles, global keyboard shortcuts, an on-air light during calls, lighting when you step away, focus sessions, phone presence and calendar rules, more customizable widgets, custom dashboard layouts, and multiple saved Hue Bridges. ### What's new in this version
```

```text
Collapse a widget. Fold any widget into a small animated moth, a pill with its name, or a round bubble, then click it to open it again or drag it somewhere else. Cartoon is the free moth; Mote Pro adds Low poly, Pixel art, Paper, and Watercolor. Choose which way the moth faces, or hold it still.

Choose how a widget folds and opens: stack its cards one by one or fade the whole panel, pick which way it expands, including evenly around the collapsed widget, and let auto collapse fold it away once the pointer leaves.

Creating a widget is new too. The Configure step is a live preview: click a card to choose its scenes or toggles, or drag it to reorder. A new Appearance step sets the theme, size, corners, collapsed look, and folding, and the preview folds just as the widget will on your desktop.

Widget settings now show one widget at a time, in clear sections. With Mote Pro, open and collapsed sizes are set separately. Control settings open in a side panel, and removing a control asks first. Pinned widgets stay bare until you hover the top edge.

Fixed: identifying a light could leave it breathing and flashing without end. It now stops after 1.5 seconds.
```
