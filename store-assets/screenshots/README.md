# Microsoft Store screenshots

Upload the files in `final/` in this order:

1. `01-control-dashboard.png` — Control your Hue lights, rooms, zones, and scenes from one customizable desktop dashboard.
2. `02-pc-sync.png` — Sync your lights with video, games, and music playing on your PC with Mote Pro.
3. `03-desktop-widgets.png` — Pin a compact lighting controller to your desktop for instant access to lights and scenes.
4. `04-custom-widgets.png` — Build custom widgets with the controls, scenes, layout, and size that fit your desktop.

All final images are 1920×1080 PNG files. `raw/` contains the live app captures used to build them. Re-run `scripts/build-store-screenshots.py` after replacing any raw capture.
